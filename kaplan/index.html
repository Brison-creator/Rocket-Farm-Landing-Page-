Where to eat in Kaplan.

Plate lunches before a shift, boiled crawfish in season, donuts on the way to the pad. Everything here is a few minutes from the gate, and most of it is on 1st Street or Veterans Memorial Drive. Hours change and small places close for holidays and hunting season, so tap a map link and call ahead.
