Flight plan

Built in stages, on land already under contract.

Three adjoining parcels on Farmer Road, developed one at a time as demand shows up. Phase 1 stands on its own — everything after it is an addition.

Phase 1 · targeted 2027

The campground

  • 17 full-hookup pull-through spaces
  • Three interior roads, gated entrance
  • Metered power, looped water, on-site treatment
  • Picnic table, fire ring and patio per space
  • North-line berm and privacy hedge

$650–$700 / month · $55 / night

Phase 2 · next door

Pond lodging & the store

  • Four 460 sf A-frame pods facing the pond, each with a covered porch and a queen loft
  • An elevated 3BR / 2BA cabin, 12 ft up, with a pond-facing deck
  • Six more full-hookup RV spaces — 23 in total
  • 800 sf general store: ice, supplies and private showers
  • Car-free, lighted walk through the pod zone

Planned: pods from $149 / night · cabin from $285 / night

Later · the big pond

The view deck

  • The large pond kept as open amenity land
  • A levee walk around the water
  • A 20 ft view deck — the spot to watch from
  • Lighting only; left quiet on purpose
