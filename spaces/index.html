At every space

Full hookups, level pads and room for a big rig.

Every space gets the same pad and the same hookups. The only real difference between them is electrical service: eleven are wired for 50-amp rigs, six for 30-amp.

Every space has

  • Separately metered 50/30/20 or 30/20 amp GFCI pedestal
  • Water and sewer at the site
  • A 13 × 70 ft angled pull-through pad on compacted limestone
  • Free Starlink satellite Wi-Fi
  • Gated entrance, lighted interior roads and trash service

Not in Phase 1

  • Picnic tables and fire rings
  • Laundry, showers and ice — they come with the general store
  • Propane delivery

Rates are being finalized. Get on the list and you’ll hear them first, along with the park rules and the monthly site agreement, before you commit to anything. Monthly stays need a current registration and insurance on the rig. Quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM. Get on the list →
