Monthly rates assume a 30-day minimum stay and a current registration and insurance on the rig. Rates shown are the planned opening rates for Phase 1 and are not yet a contract — they remain subject to final construction cost, utility rates and permitting. Reserve a space and we’ll confirm your space and rate in writing before you commit to anything. A $250 deposit reserves your space and is credited to your first month. It is not refundable if you choose to stay somewhere else. If Rocket Farm does not open, or cannot deliver the space you reserved, your deposit is refunded in full. Rent is due on the 1st and quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM; the full park rules and site agreement come with every reservation reply.
