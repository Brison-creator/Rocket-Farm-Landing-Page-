The RV spaces

Full-hookup RV sites near Starbase Louisiana.

Phase 1 is RV spaces with water, sewer and separately metered power at every site, free Starlink Wi-Fi and a gated entrance, about 30 miles from the announced launch site at Pecan Island. The final layout is being drawn now; get on the list and you’ll see it first.

At every space

Full hookups, level pads and room for a big rig.

Every space gets the same hookups: water, sewer and separately metered power, with 50-amp and 30-amp service.

Every space has

  • Separately metered 50-amp or 30-amp power
  • Water and sewer at the site
  • A compacted limestone pad
  • Free Starlink satellite Wi-Fi
  • Gated entrance, lighted interior roads and trash service

Not in Phase 1

  • Picnic tables and fire rings
  • Laundry, showers and ice — they come with the general store
  • Propane delivery

Rates are being finalized. Get on the list and you’ll hear them first, along with the park rules and the monthly site agreement, before you commit to anything. Monthly stays need a current registration and insurance on the rig. Quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM. Get on the list →
