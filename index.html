Crew manifest

Monthly RV spaces for crews first. Everybody else welcome.

Fourteen of the seventeen spaces are set aside for people working in the area on a monthly basis — that is who this park is for today. The three flexible spaces handle launch weeks, fishing trips and family stays without ever taking a space out from under someone who lives here.

Crews & contractors

Monthly spaces for craft labor, supervisors, vendors and relocating employees. Level pull-through pads you park once and leave. Power metered per space, so a bunkhouse rig and a single-unit trailer don't pay the same bill. Gated and quiet, because a night shift has to sleep in the daytime.

14 monthly spaces · from $650 + electric

Launch watchers

A dark-sky spot to park for launch week, well clear of the road closures and the crowds at the gate. Delays are normal, so the park is built for people who plan a few nights, not a few hours.

3 nightly spaces · pods & cabin in Phase 2

Sportsmen & families

Vermilion Parish runs on year-round coastal fishing and nationally known duck and goose hunting. Redfish, flounder and speckled trout in reach, public launches close by, and room to clean your catch.

Range & approach

10729 Farmer Road, Kaplan, Louisiana.

  • About 30 miles from the announced Starbase Louisiana site. The planned launch campus is at Pecan Island, roughly thirty miles due south. Near enough for a daily commute, far enough that launch-day road closures don't box you in.
  • Real town, five minutes out. Kaplan has groceries, fuel, hardware and a hospital. Lafayette is an easy drive for an airport or a night out.
  • High and dry. FEMA Zone X, outside the mapped special flood hazard area.
