Who it's for

Three kinds of people pull in off Farmer Road.

The park is laid out to serve all three without any of them getting in each other's way — long-term crews on monthly spaces, short-stay visitors on the flexible ones, and folks here for the water and the marsh.

Crews & contractors

Monthly spaces for craft labor, supervisors, vendors and relocating employees — level pull-through pads you can park a rig on and leave it, ten minutes from a real grocery store instead of an hour.

14 monthly spaces · from $650 + electric

Launch watchers

A dark-sky spot to park for launch week, well clear of the road closures and the crowds at the gate. Delays are normal, so the park is built for people who plan a few nights, not a few hours.

3 nightly spaces · pods & cabin in Phase 2

Sportsmen & families

Vermilion Parish runs on year-round coastal fishing and nationally known duck and goose hunting. Redfish, flounder and speckled trout in reach, public launches close by, and room to clean your catch.

Open season, every season

Location

10729 Farmer Road, Kaplan, Louisiana.

  • Close to the work, not in the middle of it. Roughly thirty miles north of the planned launch campus — near enough for a daily commute, far enough that road closures on launch day don't box you in.
  • Real town, five minutes out. Kaplan has groceries, fuel, hardware and a hospital. You're not driving an hour for a gallon of milk.
  • Sportsman's paradise, literally. Vermilion Parish markets year-round coastal fishing and nationally known duck and goose hunting, with public boat launches and guides nearby.
  • High and dry. The property sits in FEMA Zone X, unshaded — outside the mapped special flood hazard area.
  • Acadiana, for real. Rice fields, crawfish ponds and the kind of quiet you can hear. Lafayette is an easy drive for an airport or a night out.
