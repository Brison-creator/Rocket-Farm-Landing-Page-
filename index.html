Phase 1 · Where it starts

Starting simple, so the crews have a place to call home.

Before the cabins and the telescopes, there’s work to do on this coast — and the people doing it need somewhere decent to live. So Phase 1 is basic on purpose: full-hookup RV spaces with metered power, water and sewer at every site, free Starlink Wi-Fi and a gated entrance. Most are monthly spaces for crews; a few stay flexible for launch weeks, fishing trips and family stays.

Looking for a premium long-term RV park near Kaplan, LA? Bring your rig to Rocket Farm. Located on Farmer Road just 30 miles from the pad, our dedicated contractor campground offers spacious monthly camper spots with full-hookup RV sites, metered electric power, and level pull-through pads. Stay closely connected with high-speed, free Starlink Wi-Fi while enjoying a quiet setting that is a quick 5-minute commute to downtown Kaplan groceries, restaurants, and conveniences. Lock in your crew's housing today.

Crews & contractors

Monthly spaces for craft labor, supervisors, vendors and relocating employees. Level pull-through pads you park once and leave. Power metered per space, so a bunkhouse rig and a single-unit trailer don't pay the same bill. Gated and quiet, because a night shift has to sleep in the daytime.

Near Starbase Louisiana

An RV park about 30 miles from Starbase Louisiana.

10729 Farmer Road, Kaplan, in Vermilion Parish — close enough for a daily commute to the coast, with a real town five minutes away.

  • About 30 miles from the announced Starbase Louisiana site. The planned launch campus is at Pecan Island, roughly thirty miles due south. Near enough for a daily commute, far enough that launch-day road closures don't box you in.
  • Real town, five minutes out. Kaplan has groceries, fuel, hardware and a hospital, and more good places to eat than a town this size has any right to. See where to eat → Lafayette is an easy drive for an airport or a night out.
  • High and dry. FEMA Zone X, outside the mapped special flood hazard area.

Who we are

A family of seven, not a corporation.

We love Louisiana. Rocket Farm is our family’s project — not a fund, not a chain, not a corporation. When you call, you get us.

Our motto is in French, for the Cajun country we’re building in: le port d’attache au cosmos — your home port to the cosmos. Somewhere to come back to, under a sky full of stars.

We embrace the culture of South Louisiana — the Cajun French in our motto, the rice fields out past the fence line, boudin for breakfast, crawfish when it’s in season, and Cajun and zydeco music on a Saturday night.
