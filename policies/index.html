This document governs use and occupancy of Rocket Farm LLC property. It applies to registered occupants and, where relevant, their household members, children, visitors, invitees, contractors, pets, vehicles, RVs, and personal property. The registered occupant is responsible for making sure everyone they bring onto the property follows these rules.

Rocket Farm LLC may adopt reasonable temporary safety or operational rules when conditions require them. Nothing in this agreement is intended to waive any right or obligation that cannot lawfully be waived.

1Monthly Occupancy, Renewal, Rent & Nonpayment

1.1Month-to-Month Occupancy

Occupancy is offered on a month-to-month basis. Each monthly occupancy period is a separate calendar-month period. Unless Rocket Farm LLC has accepted renewal for the next month, the current occupancy period ends at 11:59 PM on the last calendar day of the month.

Renewal does not create a guaranteed long-term right to occupy any particular site. Rocket Farm LLC may decline future renewal subject to applicable law and any required notice.

1.2Agreed Departure Date and Checkout Time

AGREED DEPARTURE DATE: THE LAST CALENDAR DAY OF EACH MONTH. AGREED CHECKOUT TIME: 11:59 PM.

The occupant specifically agrees that the departure date for each monthly occupancy period is the last calendar day of that month at 11:59 PM unless the next monthly period has been renewed and accepted by Rocket Farm LLC.

Example. A September occupancy period has an agreed departure date of September 30 at 11:59 PM. If October renewal is accepted, the new agreed departure date becomes October 31 at 11:59 PM.

1.3Rent Due Date

MONTHLY RENT IS DUE IN FULL ON THE 1ST. THERE IS NO GRACE PERIOD. IT IS LATE ON THE 2ND.

Payment must be received and successfully processed by Rocket Farm LLC no later than 11:59 PM on the 1st. A payment that is declined, returned, reversed, disputed, charged back, or otherwise not successfully collected is not considered paid.

No reminder, invoice, telephone call, text message, email, or other demand is required before the account becomes delinquent.

1.4$55 Per Day Late-Payment Penalty

Beginning at 12:00 AM on the 2nd, a $55.00 per calendar day late-payment penalty will be assessed for each calendar day the required monthly rent remains unpaid, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

  • The $55.00 penalty is in addition to the full monthly rent.
  • It is not rent and does not replace the monthly rent.
  • It does not convert the site to a $55-per-day rental.
  • It does not reduce or satisfy any future month's rent.
  • It does not purchase another day of occupancy or prevent enforcement of a default.

Example. If the monthly rent remains unpaid for ten calendar days after becoming delinquent, the full monthly rent remains due plus $550.00 in accumulated late-payment penalties, subject to applicable law.

1.5Each New Month Is a Separate Obligation

Late-payment penalties, rent, utilities, damages, or other balances from one month do not count toward the next month. If a new month begins while prior amounts remain unpaid, the new month's full rent is still separately due.

1.6Five-Day Nonpayment Rule

FIVE CONSECUTIVE DAYS UNPAID + NO WRITTEN PAYMENT PLAN APPROVED BY ROCKET FARM LLC = MATERIAL DEFAULT AND TIME TO VACATE.

Because rent becomes delinquent on the 2nd, the five delinquent days are:

  • 2nd - Day 1
  • 3rd - Day 2
  • 4th - Day 3
  • 5th - Day 4
  • 6th - Day 5

If the account remains unresolved after the fifth delinquent day, Rocket Farm LLC may begin termination and all lawful notice, removal, eviction, collection, or other procedures available under the agreement and Louisiana law beginning on the 7th.

The daily late-payment penalty does not buy extra days and does not create permission to remain delinquent.

1.7Payment Plans

Rocket Farm LLC is not required to offer or accept a payment plan. A payment plan exists only when Rocket Farm LLC expressly approves it in writing. Statements such as “I will have it Friday,” “my check is coming,” or “give me a few more days” do not create a payment plan.

An approved plan may state exact payment amounts and deadlines. Failure to comply with any required payment under an approved plan is a default of that plan.

1.8Partial Payments

Rocket Farm LLC is not required to accept partial payment. Unless Rocket Farm LLC expressly agrees otherwise in writing, a partial payment does not cure the delinquency, stop the daily penalty, create a payment plan, extend the occupancy period, or waive Rocket Farm LLC's right to recover possession.

1.9No Automatic Reinstatement or Waiver

Once Rocket Farm LLC has terminated an occupant's right to continued occupancy, tendering money does not automatically reinstate the agreement except as required by law. Acceptance of a late payment, partial payment, waived charge, extension, or other accommodation on one occasion does not require Rocket Farm LLC to provide the same accommodation again.

1.10Complete Surrender of the Site

A site is not fully vacated simply because the occupant personally leaves. Complete surrender requires removal of the RV, camper, motorhome, trailer, vehicles, boats, equipment, outdoor furniture, personal belongings, pets, and other property brought onto the site, except where Rocket Farm LLC directs otherwise.

Property left behind will be handled only as permitted by applicable law. The occupant may be responsible for lawful removal, storage, hauling, cleanup, or disposal costs.

2Reservations, Launches & Guest Access

2.1Rocket Launches Are Not Guaranteed

Rocket Farm LLC is an independent private property and is not affiliated with SpaceX, NASA, any launch provider, or any government agency unless specifically stated otherwise. Launches may be delayed, scrubbed, rescheduled, relocated, or canceled for reasons completely outside Rocket Farm LLC's control.

A reservation purchases access to the reserved Rocket Farm LLC accommodation, site, parking area, or event. It does not guarantee that a rocket will launch or that weather and visibility will be favorable.

A launch delay, scrub, cancellation, schedule change, or lack of visibility does not automatically entitle a guest to a refund, credit, or cancellation. If Rocket Farm LLC itself cancels access to the property, management will determine the applicable refund or credit based on the reservation terms and circumstances.

2.2Launch Viewing Safety

  • Remain inside designated Rocket Farm LLC viewing and guest areas.
  • Do not enter restricted government property, launch exclusion zones, closed roads, neighboring private property, or other areas not open to you.
  • Obey law enforcement, emergency personnel, launch-related restrictions, and Rocket Farm LLC safety instructions.
  • Never point lasers or high-powered lights toward aircraft, rockets, launch facilities, vehicles, or people.

2.3Parking

Park only in designated areas. Do not block roads, RV sites, gates, fire lanes, emergency access, drainage areas, neighboring property, or other guests. Launch-night parking may require a separate reservation or fee. Unauthorized vehicles may be removed where permitted by law.

2.4Visitors and Registered Occupants

Only registered occupants and authorized visitors may use Rocket Farm LLC facilities. Rocket Farm LLC may limit visitors or charge visitor/event fees during high-demand periods. The registered occupant is responsible for the behavior of everyone they invite onto the property.

2.5Cabins and RV Sites

Guests are responsible for safely operating their own RVs, vehicles, equipment, hoses, cords, surge protectors, adapters, and connections. Guests may not modify Rocket Farm LLC electrical, water, sewer, septic, structural, or utility infrastructure.

Cabin occupants may not disable safety equipment, tamper with building systems, remove property, or move indoor furniture outside without permission. Guests may be charged for damage, missing property, smoking damage, or excessive cleaning beyond ordinary use.

3Conduct, Noise & Disruption

3.1Quiet Hours

QUIET HOURS: 10:00 PM TO 7:00 AM DAILY.

During quiet hours, noise must be kept at a level that cannot reasonably be expected to disturb neighboring sites, cabins, guests, or surrounding property owners.

  • No excessively loud music, televisions, speakers, parties, yelling, or repeated horn use.
  • No unnecessary engine revving, prolonged idling, power tools, construction activity, or excessively loud generators during quiet hours.
  • Pets may not be allowed to bark or create repeated uncontrolled noise.

3.2Daytime Noise

Quiet hours do not mean unlimited noise is permitted during the day. Rocket Farm LLC may require excessive or disruptive noise to be reduced or stopped at any time.

3.3Launch Events

Occupants acknowledge that launch activity may involve rockets, aircraft, crowds, announcements, vehicles, and unusual noise at unusual hours. Normal guest-created noise rules remain in effect unless management announces a temporary event-specific exception.

3.4Disruptive Conduct

Conduct that materially interferes with the safety, peace, comfort, privacy, or reasonable enjoyment of other occupants, guests, employees, contractors, neighbors, or visitors is prohibited.

  • Fighting, violence, threats, intimidation, or aggressive confrontation.
  • Harassment or repeated targeted verbal abuse.
  • Disorderly or dangerously intoxicated behavior.
  • Reckless driving or dangerous conduct.
  • Entering another guest's site, cabin, or belongings without permission.
  • Intentional property damage or interference with another person's property.
  • Repeated disturbance after being asked to stop.
  • Refusing reasonable safety or property instructions from Rocket Farm LLC management.
  • Illegal activity.

3.5Enforcement

Minor violations may result in a verbal or written warning. Rocket Farm LLC is not required to repeatedly warn an occupant about the same conduct. Repeated violations may constitute a material breach. Serious conduct - including violence, credible threats, criminal activity, major property damage, severe harassment, or an immediate safety risk - may result in immediate action and termination where permitted by law.

Rocket Farm LLC may consider personal observation, complaints, photographs, video, security footage, police reports, written statements, and other reasonably reliable evidence when investigating violations. The identity of a complaining guest does not have to be disclosed.

4Site Cleanliness & Property Care

4.1Clean and Orderly Sites

Every occupant must keep the assigned site reasonably clean, sanitary, organized, and free from unnecessary debris. Normal outdoor living is fine. A site may not become unsanitary, hazardous, pest-attracting, excessively cluttered, obstructive, damaging to the property, or a nuisance to neighboring guests.

  • Remove household trash, food waste, cardboard, bottles, cans, broken equipment, scrap material, pet waste, construction debris, and unnecessary refuse.
  • Keep walkways, roads, utility connections, fire access, and neighboring areas clear.
  • Store ordinary camping furniture, grills, coolers, bicycles, and recreational items in a reasonably orderly way.

4.2No Outdoor Storage Yard

An RV site is not a junkyard, warehouse, mechanic shop, or long-term storage yard. Without management approval, occupants may not accumulate appliances, mattresses, scrap metal, tires, automobile parts, building materials, broken equipment, commercial inventory, unused machinery, large quantities of tools, or similar items outside.

4.3Trash and Food Waste

Household trash must be securely bagged and placed in designated receptacles. Trash may not be burned, dumped in ponds or ditches, thrown on neighboring property, or allowed to accumulate around a site. Food waste, pet food, grease, and garbage must be handled in a way that does not attract wildlife or pests.

4.4Pet Waste

Pet waste must be picked up promptly and properly disposed of. Repeated failure to clean up after an animal is a cleanliness violation.

4.5Sewage, Gray Water and Other Waste

Do not dump sewage, gray water, oil, grease, chemicals, fuel, wastewater, or other liquids onto the ground, grass, roadway, pond, drainage area, or neighboring property. Wastewater may be discharged only into an approved connection or disposal location.

4.6Vehicle and Equipment Repair

Minor emergency maintenance may be permitted. Major engine repair, vehicle dismantling, sanding, painting, welding, fluid changes, restoration work, or commercial repair activity that creates noise, fumes, debris, spills, or hazards requires prior approval.

4.7Cleanup Notices and Charges

Rocket Farm LLC may issue a cleanup notice identifying the condition to be corrected and a reasonable deadline. Immediate hazards may require immediate correction. If an occupant fails to correct a violation, or immediate action is reasonably needed to protect people or property, Rocket Farm LLC may arrange lawful cleanup and charge the occupant the reasonable actual cost of labor, hauling, disposal, pest control, equipment rental, sewage cleanup, hazardous cleanup, and resulting damage.

4.8Condition at Departure

At departure, remove all trash, personal property, furniture, RV equipment, food, pet waste, vehicles, and debris. Leaving unwanted property behind is not proper disposal.

Rocket Farm LLC will not deny service, impose different terms, provide inferior service, or otherwise treat a person differently because of a characteristic protected by applicable federal, Louisiana, or local law.

WE DO NOT DISCRIMINATE AGAINST PEOPLE BECAUSE OF WHO THEY ARE. WE ENFORCE OUR RULES BASED ON WHAT PEOPLE DO.

Protected status does not excuse nonpayment, unsafe conduct, property damage, excessive noise, harassment, sanitation violations, unauthorized occupants, fraud, or other legitimate rule violations. Likewise, Rocket Farm LLC will not selectively enforce rules against someone because of a protected characteristic.

5.2Disability Accommodations and Service Animals

Rocket Farm LLC will comply with applicable disability-access and housing laws, including reasonable modifications or accommodations when legally required. Qualifying service animals will not be treated as ordinary pets when the law requires different treatment. Handlers remain responsible for maintaining control and for damage as permitted by law.

5.3Harassment and Retaliation

Rocket Farm LLC is an actively developing property. We intend to keep improving the grounds, infrastructure, landscaping, utilities, roads, RV sites, cabins, launch-viewing areas, common spaces, and amenities.

Guests may occasionally observe construction, landscaping, mowing, grading, utility installation, deliveries, contractors, equipment, repairs, cabin or site construction, and other improvement activity.

Rocket Farm LLC will make reasonable efforts to schedule louder work during normal daytime hours, minimize unnecessary disruption, keep unsafe areas marked, maintain reasonable access to occupied sites, and respect guest privacy and comfort.

7Electrical Service & Power Outage Action Plan

7.1If Power Goes Out

  1. Check whether nearby sites, cabins, lights, or common areas also appear to have lost power.
  2. Check the breaker panel inside your RV, camper, or cabin. To reset a tripped breaker, switch it fully OFF, wait a few seconds, then switch it firmly ON once.
  3. Check the accessible breaker at your site power pedestal. If tripped, switch it fully OFF, wait several seconds, then switch it ON once.
  4. Make sure your RV power cord, adapter, surge protector, or electrical-management system is properly connected and is not showing a fault condition.
  5. Reduce electrical load by turning off high-demand appliances such as air conditioners, electric water heaters, space heaters, microwaves, hair dryers, or electric cooking appliances, then try one reset.

7.2Do Not Perform Electrical Repairs

Guests may reset accessible breakers and check normal plug connections. Guests may not remove electrical panel covers, alter wiring, replace breakers, bypass safety devices, modify power pedestals, install temporary wiring, or attempt repairs to Rocket Farm LLC electrical infrastructure.

7.3Overnight Power Issues

ROUTINE OVERNIGHT POWER PROBLEMS WILL NORMALLY BE ADDRESSED IN THE MORNING. ROCKET FARM LLC DOES NOT GUARANTEE 24-HOUR MAINTENANCE DISPATCH.

If a non-emergency electrical issue occurs during the middle of the night, complete the basic troubleshooting steps and notify Rocket Farm LLC. Please do not repeatedly call or expect overnight maintenance for a routine outage, tripped breaker, failed connection, utility outage, or similar non-emergency condition.

Immediate contact is appropriate for fire, smoke, visible sparking, electrical arcing, melted wiring, a strong burning smell, a downed electrical line, exposed energized equipment, or another condition creating an immediate risk of injury or significant property damage. Call emergency services when appropriate.

7.4Utility Interruptions

Rocket Farm LLC cannot guarantee uninterrupted electrical utility service. Temporary outages caused by utility providers, storms, grid conditions, equipment failures, accidents, guest equipment, or circumstances outside Rocket Farm LLC's reasonable control may occur. Temporary power loss does not automatically qualify an occupant for a refund, rent reduction, hotel expense, food-loss reimbursement, or other compensation.

8Water Service, Leaks & Outage Policy

8.1If Water Stops Working

  1. Determine whether neighboring sites or cabins also appear to be without water.
  2. Make sure your RV or site water connection is properly connected.
  3. Check that your individual water shutoff valve is open.
  4. Check your hoses, filters, pressure regulator, fittings, and RV equipment for obvious restrictions or problems.
  5. During freezing weather, determine whether your hose or connection may be frozen.
  6. Confirm that the problem is not inside your own RV plumbing system.

8.2Overnight Water Issues

ROUTINE OVERNIGHT WATER PROBLEMS WILL NORMALLY BE ADDRESSED IN THE MORNING. MAJOR LEAKS OR FLOODING ARE EMERGENCIES.

If a non-emergency water issue occurs in the middle of the night, perform the basic checks above and notify Rocket Farm LLC. Unless there is an active major leak, flooding, significant property damage, or immediate safety concern, Rocket Farm LLC will inspect and address the issue during normal morning maintenance hours.

8.3Water Emergencies

Immediately report major leaks, broken lines, continuous spraying water, flooding, water entering electrical equipment, a damaged water pedestal, a burst pipe, or another condition that could cause significant property damage. If an accessible individual-site shutoff can safely be closed, close it to limit damage. Do not attempt repairs beyond normal guest-accessible controls.

8.4Guest Equipment and Leaks

Occupants are responsible for maintaining their own hoses, pressure regulators, filters, fittings, connections, and internal RV plumbing. Guest equipment that leaks, wastes water, or threatens property may be disconnected or shut off. Guests may be responsible for damage or excessive use caused by their equipment, negligence, improper connections, or failure to report a known leak.

8.5Water Use Rules

  • Use an appropriate RV water-pressure regulator.
  • Do not leave water running unattended except when Rocket Farm LLC specifically directs limited flow for freeze protection.
  • Do not fill large pools, conduct commercial washing, perform unrelated irrigation, fill large tanks, or engage in unusually high-volume use without approval.
  • Do not alter plumbing, remove backflow protection, bypass shutoff valves, tap another site, or connect equipment that could contaminate the water system.
  • Report standing water, unexplained pooling, broken fittings, constant leaks, or other suspected system leaks promptly.

8.6Temporary Water Shutdowns

Rocket Farm LLC may temporarily interrupt or shut off water for repairs, leak control, construction, utility work, freezing conditions, emergencies, contamination concerns, excessive waste, system upgrades, or other legitimate operational reasons. Temporary water loss does not automatically qualify an occupant for a refund, rent reduction, hotel reimbursement, laundry reimbursement, or other compensation.

9Pets, Children, Fire, Alcohol, Weapons & Drones

9.1Children

Children must be supervised by a responsible adult, especially around roads, moving vehicles, water, drainage areas, wildlife, utilities, equipment, construction, fires, and uneven terrain. Rocket Farm LLC is not a childproof environment.

9.2Pets

Pets must remain under the owner's control. Aggressive animals are not permitted. Owners must clean up pet waste and prevent repeated barking or nuisance behavior. Pets should not be left outside unattended for extended periods. Owners are responsible for damage or injury caused by their animals as permitted by law.

9.3Fires and Grills

Fires are permitted only in designated fire pits or approved cooking equipment and may be restricted because of burn bans, high winds, drought, or unsafe conditions. Never leave a fire unattended. Fireworks, explosives, homemade rockets, flares, or similar devices are prohibited unless Rocket Farm LLC has specifically authorized a controlled event.

9.4Alcohol

Responsible alcohol consumption by persons of legal drinking age is permitted unless otherwise posted. Public intoxication, fighting, impaired driving, threatening conduct, or behavior that creates a safety problem is prohibited.

9.5Weapons

Guests must comply with all applicable weapons laws. Discharge of firearms, recreational shooting, or use of weapons on Rocket Farm LLC property is prohibited unless specifically authorized for a lawful designated activity.

9.6Drones

Drone operation is not automatically permitted simply because a person is staying at Rocket Farm LLC. Operators are responsible for FAA requirements, temporary flight restrictions, launch-related restrictions, privacy laws, and other applicable rules. Rocket Farm LLC may prohibit drone operation at any time. Never operate a drone in a way that interferes with aircraft, launch operations, emergency personnel, neighboring property, or other guests.

10Weather, Emergencies & Natural Conditions

10.1Weather

South Louisiana weather can change quickly. Rain, lightning, high winds, thunderstorms, tropical weather, tornado warnings, flooding, heat, and standing water may affect the property. Guests are responsible for monitoring weather and following emergency instructions.

Rocket Farm LLC may temporarily close areas, restrict movement, require sheltering, relocate operations, or require evacuation when management reasonably believes conditions are unsafe.

10.2Hurricanes, Tropical Weather and Emergencies

Rocket Farm LLC may cancel or modify reservations, events, parking, or property access because of hurricanes, tropical systems, flooding, mandatory evacuation orders, utility failures, or other emergencies. Guest safety takes priority over reservations. Refunds or credits caused by a Rocket Farm LLC closure will be handled according to the reservation terms and circumstances.

11Stay at Your Own Risk - Louisiana Marsh Safety Notice

Welcome to Rocket Farm LLC. We are glad you are here. But understand where you are: this is rural South Louisiana marsh country. It is beautiful, wild, wet, hot, muddy, buggy, unpredictable, and occasionally something with teeth may be looking back at you.

CAJUN GRANDPA RULE: WATCH WHERE YOU PUT YOUR FEET, DO NOT PET ANYTHING YOU DID NOT BRING WITH YOU, AND IF THE WATER STARTS BLINKING AT YOU - BACK UP.

11.1Alligators

Alligators may live in or travel through ponds, ditches, canals, marshes, drainage areas, fields, standing water, or other wet areas. Do not approach, feed, chase, touch, or attempt to photograph an alligator from close range. Keep children and pets away from water edges unless directly supervised.

IF YOU ARE WONDERING WHETHER YOU ARE TOO CLOSE TO AN ALLIGATOR, YOU PROBABLY ARE.

11.2Snakes

Guests may encounter snakes, including species that may be dangerous. Do not attempt to identify a snake by picking it up. Stop, give it room, keep children and pets away, and notify management if necessary.

11.3Bugs, Fire Ants and Other Wildlife

Mosquitoes, fire ants, wasps, bees, spiders, flies, gnats, ticks, beetles, frogs, raccoons, armadillos, possums, coyotes, wild hogs, nutria, rodents, birds, turtles, stray animals, and other wildlife may be present. Bug spray and reasonable protective clothing are strongly recommended.

Do not feed wildlife. Feeding wild animals can make them lose their natural fear of people and create a dangerous condition.

Fire-ant mounds should be avoided. If you accidentally stand in one, you will understand this rule immediately.

11.4Water, Ponds, Ditches and Marsh Areas

Do not assume any pond, ditch, canal, marsh, or standing water is safe for swimming, wading, playing, fishing, or pets. Water depth can change, banks may be unstable, mud may be deeper than it looks, and wildlife may be present even when you cannot see it. Unless Rocket Farm LLC specifically designates an area for recreational use, stay out of the water.

11.5Mud and Soft Ground

South Louisiana ground can go from firm to soup after heavy rain. Remain on designated roads, pads, parking areas, and walking areas whenever practical. Do not drive through wet fields or unauthorized areas because they “look solid.” Louisiana mud has embarrassed bigger trucks than yours. Guests may be responsible for damage or recovery costs caused by entering restricted or obviously unsuitable areas.

11.6Uneven Ground

Guests may encounter holes, roots, ruts, gravel, soft spots, ditches, wet grass, mud, changes in elevation, and other naturally uneven surfaces. Watch where you walk and use appropriate footwear.

11.7Heat and Humidity

Louisiana heat and humidity can be extreme. Drink water, use shade, take breaks, monitor children and vulnerable guests, and know your limits. Heat-related illness can become serious quickly.

11.8Lightning, Floodwater and Severe Weather

During lightning, seek appropriate shelter and leave open fields and elevated viewing areas. Floodwater may contain sewage, chemicals, debris, sharp objects, unstable ground, or wildlife. Do not drive through water when you cannot determine the depth or condition underneath.

11.9Nighttime Safety

Wildlife is often more active after dark and visibility is worse. Carry a flashlight, remain on established paths, keep pets close, avoid tall grass and water edges, and pay attention to where you step.

TWO LITTLE GLOWING EYES NEAR THE WATER ARE NOT AN INVITATION TO GO INVESTIGATE.

11.10Natural Conditions Are Part of the Experience

Rocket Farm LLC cannot guarantee that an outdoor rural property will be free of wildlife, insects, mud, rain, standing water, heat, humidity, wind, dust, natural vegetation, uneven ground, or similar rural conditions. Guests voluntarily choose to use the property with knowledge that normal outdoor and marshland hazards may be present.

11.11Use Common Sense

If something looks dangerous, feels unstable, has teeth, is hissing, is buzzing angrily, is flooded, is sparking, is smoking, or has a sign telling you not to go there - leave it alone and notify management when appropriate.
